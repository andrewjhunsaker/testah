## Fix 1 — positional FAQ addressing (pricing-faq-accordion.spec.ts:14-30)

**Resolved.** The loop now addresses `pricing.faqItemAt(index)` and asserts
`summaryOf(item)` `toHaveText(entry.question)`, so slot *n* must be question
*n*. `index` is no longer decorative.

Probed the DOM directly: `main` holds exactly **4** `role=group` nodes, each a
real `<DETAILS>` with `open=false`, exactly **1** `<p>`, and `summary` innerText
equal to the question with nothing appended — so `toHaveText` (exact, not
substring) is the right strength and `toHaveJSProperty('open', …)` is reading
the `<details>` itself, not a wrapper:

    [0] <DETAILS> open=false p=1 "What do I get after purchase?"
    [1] <DETAILS> open=false p=1 "Is this a monthly subscription?"
    [2] <DETAILS> open=false p=1 "How quickly is the visibility scoreboard delivered?"
    [3] <DETAILS> open=false p=1 "Is my payment and business data secure?"

Teeth confirmed rather than assumed: I swapped items 0 and 1 in the live DOM and
re-ran the assertion — it failed in 2.0s with
`Expected "What do I get after purchase?" / Received "Is this a monthly
subscription?"`. Round 1's complaint (any permutation passes) is genuinely gone.

Scenario 1 coverage is complete and nothing was lost in the swap: `h2 "FAQ"`
visible (L11), exactly four items (L12), the four summaries **in this order**
(L17-21), each collapsed (L22-25), each answer hidden (L26-29). Positional
addressing is strictly stronger than the by-question form it replaced — it pins
identity *and* order in one assertion.

## Fix 2 & 3 — self-contained `toPass` callbacks (help-card-grid.spec.ts:64-70, pricing-primary-cta.spec.ts:37-43)

**Resolved, and no new problems.** I reproduced both regression shapes live
against staging:

- **Card `href` regressed** (expected path the card will never reach): fails in
  **15.0s** — inside the 15s `toPass` budget, well under the 30s test timeout —
  with `Expected pattern /\/help\/getting-started-REGRESSED$/ / Received
  "https://staging.vizaeo.com/help/getting-started"`. That is exactly the
  diagnostic the criterion wants, pointing at the route.
- **Card missing from the grid**: fails in **15.0s** with `locator.click:
  Timeout 3000ms exceeded — waiting for getByRole('main').getByRole('link', {
  name: … })`, naming the missing card. The bounded `click({ timeout: 3_000 })`
  is what keeps this inside the budget.
- **CTA route regressed**: fails in **15.0s** with `Expected
  ".../signup-REGRESSED" / Received ".../signup"`. Same clean shape.

On the two questions the Steward asked:

**Does `goto()` inside the callback weaken teeth?** No — it strengthens them
slightly. Each attempt now re-establishes the criterion's own Given ("an
anonymous visitor on `/help`" / "on `/pricing`"), so every retry re-tests the
stated scenario instead of whatever state the previous attempt left behind. It
also closes a masking hole: a defect where the *first* click after every page
load is swallowed would have been hidden by the round-1 form (second click, same
document, succeeds) and is now caught, because attempt 2 starts from a fresh
load too. All nine routing tests and the CTA test passed on their first attempt
live (3.0–3.7s each), so the retry is not load-bearing in the happy path.

**Does it introduce anything?** Nothing blocking. Worst-case runtime is bounded
at ~15s per test against a 30s timeout, confirmed three times. The one behavior
change worth the Author knowing: because every attempt reloads, the retry only
absorbs an *intermittent* hydration swallow, not a load-invariant one — which is
the correct trade (a persistent defect should be red, not slow). The round-1
evidence showed the swallow is intermittent.

## Notes (non-blocking — no verdict impact)

- **Round 1's stated mechanism for fixes 2 and 3 was wrong, though its verdict
  was still right.** It argued the non-idempotent callback would "block straight
  past the 15s `toPass` budget until the 30s test timeout" because "`toPass`
  cannot interrupt an in-flight callback". It can: `pollAgainstDeadline`
  (`playwright-core/lib/coreBundle.js:4285-4305`, 1.62.1) calls
  `raceAgainstDeadline(wrappedCallback, deadline)` and abandons an in-flight
  callback at the deadline. I ran the round-1 form as a control and it also
  failed at **15.2s** with the URL mismatch, not at 30s. I verified round 1's
  supporting observation independently and it does hold — on
  `/help/getting-started`, `main` has 9 links and **0** named "Getting Started"
  — so the retried click really did hang; `toPass` just cut it loose. Recording
  this so the reasoning is not carried forward as fact. The fixes are still net
  positive on their own merits (correct Given per attempt, no unbounded
  orphaned click left running past the deadline, and the missing-card case now
  fails with a named locator instead of a raced-out callback), so I am not
  reverting the ask.
- Round 1's accepted non-blocking notes still stand and are not re-litigated:
  the `.locator('p')` / `.locator('summary')` raw-selector justifications
  (re-confirmed: 1 `<p>` per collapsed item), the HTTP-status assertions being
  paired with a load-bearing `toHaveURL`, the deliberately pinned Deep Reports
  gap, `SignupPage` having no page-map (Steward item), the POM-less
  `/dashboard` control, and the redundant `not.toHaveText('')`.
- Craft re-checked across the whole batch: `implements:` header on all four
  specs; POM per page in `tests/pages/`; `getByRole` throughout with the only
  two raw selectors carrying justifying comments; no `waitForTimeout`, no
  `sleep`, no `test.only`/`test.skip`, no vacuous `toBeDefined`/`toBeTruthy`; no
  `@flaky` or `@seed-failure` tags; no shared mutable state and no order
  dependence between tests (all 33 pass at 4 workers with `fullyParallel`).
